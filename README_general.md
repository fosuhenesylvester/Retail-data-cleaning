## How It's Organized

Each skill area has its own folder. Inside each folder, individual attempts or exercises have their own subfolder and their own `README.md`, covering:

* What the task was
* What I did
* What went right
* What went wrong, and what I learned from it
* Any relevant materials, datasets, or working files

Scores, specific mistakes, and technical breakdowns live inside each attempt's own README — not here. Check the relevant folder for that level of detail.

## Principles I Try to Follow, Across Every Skill Area

* Preserve the raw data — never start by destroying original evidence
* Understand the data before transforming it
* Verify a result before trusting it — a working formula or output isn't proof it's correct
* Flag uncertain or suspicious items instead of silently deleting or guessing
* Keep a trail a reviewer could follow without needing me to explain it
* Connect the technical work back to what it's actually meant to answer

## Why I Publish the Mistakes, Not Just the Wins

It would be easier to only show polished results. I don't, because the mistakes are part of what shows this is a real learning process rather than a highlight reel. Each attempt folder includes what didn't work the first time, alongside what did.
